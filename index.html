📐 The rubric — how every speech is scored (1–5)

Speech & interview criteria (準1級 面接)

Pronunciation 発音 — 1: errors often block understanding · 3: noticeable slips but always clear · 5: natural stress, linking and rhythm.
Fluency 流暢さ — 1: frequent long pauses · 3: some hesitation, recovers without freezing · 5: smooth, natural pace start to finish.
Grammar 文法 — 1: basic errors obscure meaning · 3: solid base, slips only in complex forms · 5: accurate, varied 準1級-level structures.
Vocabulary 語彙 — 1: simple words only · 3: some 準1級 items used correctly · 5: rich, precise, natural word choice.
Content & interaction 内容 — 1: ideas thin or off-topic · 3: clear opinion with support · 5: organized, persuasive, answers questions directly (concession + reason).

Video delivery criteria

Camera presence — eyes up, talking to someone · Structure — signposts guide the listener · Memorization ownership — the speech sounds like her own words, not recitation · Pacing — 3–5 minutes, no racing.
